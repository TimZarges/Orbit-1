`Intelligent training control & analytics for triathlon, cycling, running & swim.`

### Full Description (max 4,000 chars)
```markdown
ORBIT is the modern, focused training platform for endurance athletes and coaches. From day-to-day session planning and in-depth telemetry analysis to privacy-first collaboration between athlete and coach.

CORE PRINCIPLE: SIMPLE FIRST, DETAILS ONE LEVEL DEEPER
• Readiness at a glance: The Volt Daily Ring and physiological form indicator show your current training capacity immediately.
• Science-backed load modeling: CTL (Fitness), ATL (Fatigue), and TSB (Form) calculated precisely from your personal thresholds.
• Automatic plan vs. actual matching: Completed activities seamlessly pair with scheduled workouts.

• Direct Garmin Connect integration & instant FIT file import.

FOR COACHES:
• High-performance Desktop & Web Dashboard featuring a multi-athlete week grid.
• Rapid workout scheduling with Drag & Drop (Alt-copy) and full Undo history.

• O(1) Architecture: Lightning-fast roster views for 25+ athletes.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN:
• Hosted strictly in the EU (Frankfurt, europe-west3).

Reach your peak performance with ORBIT.
```
